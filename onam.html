ONAM

Onam is one of the most important and colorful festivals celebrated in Kerala. It is celebrated to welcome King Mahabali, who is believed to visit Kerala during this festival. People celebrate Onam with great happiness and enthusiasm by cleaning and decorating their homes, wearing new clothes, and preparing delicious traditional food. A beautiful flower decoration called Pookalam is made in front of houses. The grand vegetarian feast called Onam Sadya is another major attraction of the festival. Traditional games, dances, cultural programs, and the famous Vallam Kali or snake boat race are also part of the celebrations. Onam brings families and friends together and spreads the message of love, unity, happiness, and prosperity. It also showcases the rich culture and traditions of Kerala.
