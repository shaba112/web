INDIAN FESTIVALS

  • DIWALI

Diwali is the major Hindu festival of lights that celebrates the spiritual victory of light over darkness, good over evil, and knowledge over ignorance.

  • ONAM

Onam is the official 10-day harvest and cultural festival of Kerala, India, celebrated annually during the Malayalam month of Chingam (August–September).

  • HOLI

Holi is a Festival of Colours.Holi is a major Hindu spring festival known as the Festival of Colors that celebrates love, renewal, and the triumph of good over evil.
