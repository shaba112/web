DIWALI

Diwali, also known as Deepavali or the Festival of Lights, is one of the most important and widely celebrated festivals in India. It is celebrated with great joy and enthusiasm by people of different communities. Diwali symbolizes the victory of light over darkness, good over evil, and knowledge over ignorance. Before the festival, people clean and decorate their homes with colourful rangoli, flowers, candles, diyas, and beautiful lights. On the main day of Diwali, families wear new and traditional clothes and perform Lakshmi Puja to pray for happiness, prosperity, and success. People prepare delicious traditional sweets and dishes and share them with their family, friends, and neighbours. Children especially enjoy the festival by receiving gifts and lighting fireworks, while many people now prefer eco-friendly celebrations to protect the environment. Diwali is also a time when people forget their differences, meet their loved ones, and spread love, kindness, and happiness. The bright lights, beautiful decorations, tasty sweets, and cheerful atmosphere make Diwali a very special and memorable festival for everyone.
